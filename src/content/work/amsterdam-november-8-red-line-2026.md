On November 8, 2026, protesters took part in a “Red Line” demonstration in Amsterdam against what organisers call a genocide in Gaza. Organised by Amnesty International, Oxfam Novib, PAX and around 150 other organisations, the march started and ended at Museumplein. Organisers demand that the Netherlands cut military and economic ties with Israel, use political and economic pressure to end the illegal occupation of the Palestinian territories, and implement the rulings of the International Court of Justice (ICJ). It is the fourth Red Line protest in the Netherlands and the second in Amsterdam, after an estimated 250,000 people took part in October 2025.
