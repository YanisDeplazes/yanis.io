This is where Groundswell started. On 11 October 2025, I went to Bern with a film camera, for an unauthorised national pro-Palestinian demonstration called under the slogan "2 Jahre Genozid – 100 Jahre Widerstand".

Thousands came, and around 600 police officers from across Switzerland were waiting. Police kettled demonstrators near the Federal Palace, and the day ended in clashes, with injuries and heavy damage in the city centre. By the end of the day, police had detained 536 people. On film, every frame counted.
