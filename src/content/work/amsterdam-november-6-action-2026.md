On November 6, 2026, activists from the Dutch group Geef Tegengas occupied the construction site of a Microsoft data centre in Amsterdam, climbing a crane and the roof of the building. They object to its power use and to Microsoft’s ties to Israel. The mayor banned the action and riot police intervened.
