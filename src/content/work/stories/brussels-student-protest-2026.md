On November 17, 2026, French-speaking students marched through Brussels against a rise in tuition fees and cuts to education, joined by unions. Outside the government building of the Wallonia-Brussels Federation they chanted, drummed and hung banners on the barbed wire. At a closed road tunnel riot police blocked the march, pushed protesters back and detained several of them.
