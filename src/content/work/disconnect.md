**Disconnect** grew out of my residency in Amsterdam with <a href='https://www.instagram.com/thick.collective/' target='_blank'>Thick Collective</a>. It was first commissioned for a night at A'DAM Tower and has since been shown at FC Hyena and OT301.

The visuals run alongside live performances: footage I filmed, then reworked in TouchDesigner and After Effects around one theme, disconnection in a digital world.
