**High Gain** documents punk and hardcore across Europe, show by show. Small rooms, very little light, a lot of noise.

The bands are only half of it. These scenes run on the people who book the shows, work the door, stand behind the mixing desk and hold up the person next to them in the pit. This series is for them.
