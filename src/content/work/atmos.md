**Atmos** listens to its surroundings. It reads environmental data and answers in light, sound and a holographic projection, so the data around us becomes something you can see and feel.

A prototype built during my Internet of Things exchange semester at the Amsterdam University of Applied Sciences, where embedded hardware and a web interface had to work as one installation.
