On 14 June 2026, the day before the G7 summit opened in Évian, just across the border, thousands marched through Geneva with the NoG7 coalition. Police called the security operation unprecedented in Switzerland.

The march was largely peaceful through the afternoon. By the evening, clashes broke out, and police fired tear gas and water cannon and broke up the demonstration.
