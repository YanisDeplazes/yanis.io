On the evening of 17 September 2026, a pro-Israel rally, "Op de bres voor Israël", took place on Dam Square in Amsterdam. The Amsterdam Palestina Coalitie had announced a counter-protest on the Dam, but the mayor moved it to the Oude Turfmarkt, around 600 metres away, citing the risk of public disorder. The coalition challenged the decision in court the same day, and the judge upheld it.

Demonstrators gathered at the Oude Turfmarkt, where police encircled them.
