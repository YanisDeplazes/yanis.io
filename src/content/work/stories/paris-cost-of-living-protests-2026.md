In autumn 2026, high school students in Paris blocked their schools to demand better conditions for their education and their future. Workers, trade unions and Yellow Vests joined them in a series of national days of protest against the high cost of living. This story follows two of those days, from police kettles in the early morning to barricades, flares and street medics at nightfall.
