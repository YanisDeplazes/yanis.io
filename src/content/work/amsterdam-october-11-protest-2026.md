On 10 October 2025, a ceasefire was declared in Gaza. A year later, protesters gathered on Dam Square for a national protest organised by Plant een Olijfboom and the Palestinian Community Netherlands (PGNL), under the slogan "Three years of genocide is not a ceasefire."
