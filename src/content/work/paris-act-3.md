For the third weekend in a row young people took to the streets against the high cost of living and the state of schools. This series follows the day from the start of the march with its signs and Halloween costumes to the flares and standoffs with police at Porte de Charenton. It ends at nightfall with street medics and a car set on fire on Avenue Charles de Foucauld.
