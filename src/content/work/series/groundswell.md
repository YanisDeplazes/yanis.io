**Groundswell** is where my protest work lives. It began at a national pro-Palestinian protest in Bern in October 2025 and has since followed people onto the streets of Zurich, Geneva, Amsterdam, Brussels and Paris.

I am interested in what holds a crowd together as much as in the moment it breaks: the waiting, the homemade signs, the people who look after each other. Below is a selection across all of it. Longer stories are filed underneath; my day-to-day news coverage is distributed through Hans Lucas.
