For weeks high school students had been blocking their schools to demand better conditions for their education and their future. On October 17, 2026 workers answered their call and joined them in a national day of protest against the high cost of living. This series follows young protesters in Paris from a police kettle in the early morning to the crowds and barricades around Place de la Republique. It ends with the clashes with police and the street medics caring for the injured.
